# Changelog

## 0.5.0
- Hidden star charts. Every star hides one on one of its planets or moons: a small canister half-buried far from where you land.
- The scanner won't tag it from a distance. On the right world it picks up a faint signal and tells you whether it's near or a long way out; once you're within 25 m it shows up. Walk onto it to pick it up.
- Find every star chart on a journey and the core offers a new trip: travel on to a new galaxy. Your ship, suit upgrades and cargo come with you.
- New galaxies have an extra star, 4 or 5 planets round each star, and slightly harsher worlds. They hide their own star charts, so you can keep going, galaxy after galaxy.
- The star pips show a diamond for each star whose chart you've found, and the orbit screen counts your charts.
- The home screen says which galaxy a saved journey is in, and how many new galaxies you've reached.
- A star's card now counts its moons separately from its planets.

## 0.4.0
- No more tapping to mine or scan. Walk up to a rock, plant or crystal and you mine it; walk near a creature you haven't named and you scan it. Tapping something still walks you over to it.
- Every planet has goals: 3 to 5 things to do there, like naming all its species, searching its wrecks and pods, mining its black spires, and one or two things special to that world (fly over a cliff, shelter in a cave, reach the edge of the map, gather a resource, or shoot down a watcher).
- The next goal shows under your meters, with a count of how many are done. Tap it to see them all.
- Finish every goal to chart the planet and pick a suit upgrade. (Before, naming every species gave the upgrade.)
- On planets with more than one species, one of them is rare: a lone animal living far from your ship.
- The star chart no longer shows names, so planets aren't covered up. Tap one for its card, which now lists its goals. Charted planets wear a small tick.

## 0.3.1
- Tapping a boulder, cliff or big tree walks you up to its near side and stops there. Before, you'd walk into it and jitter left and right on the spot.
- Walking round something big now keeps going round one side instead of changing its mind every step.
- If you tap somewhere you can't get to, you give up after a couple of seconds of getting no closer.

## 0.3.0
- A jetpack. Press and hold anywhere to lift off, and drag while you hold to fly: faster than walking, and straight over boulders, pools and trees.
- It lasts about 2.5 seconds in the air, then needs to land. A small gauge beside you shows how much is left, and it refills quickly on the ground.
- A ring fills under your thumb while you hold, so you can see the jetpack about to fire. A quick tap still means "go there".
- On a computer, hold Shift or J.

## 0.2.0
- Planets have a lie of the land now: groves of big trees, rocky fields of boulders, flat-topped cliffs, and pools of water, acid, tar or ice. You walk round them, and so do the creatures.
- The big trees suit the weather: leafy on calm worlds, palms in storms, bare and dead where it's scorched, snowy pines where it's frozen, giant mushrooms where it's toxic.
- The ground is busier too: flowers, dunes, bones, snow drifts, puddles and spores, depending on the planet.
- Places to find on every planet. Wrecks can be salvaged for ferrite and warp shards, supply pods hold carbon and sodium, monoliths tell you a little about the core, and caves shelter you from bad weather like your ship does.
- Birds fly over planets with life on them.
- Star systems have 3 or 4 planets, and some planets have a small moon you can land on. Moons have no air and no life, but plenty of rock and warp shards.
- Every star has a trading station. Dock to swap spare carbon, sodium and ferrite for warp shards, buy fuel, or buy one suit upgrade.
- A planet's card in orbit tells you what places there are to find on it.
- The scanner tags places to find, and walking close to one shows its name.

## 0.1.0
- First version. You crash-land on a planet nobody has seen, with an empty fuel tank and the galaxy's core four stars away.
- Every planet is made up the moment you land: its weather, its ground, its plants and the creatures that live there, all drawn in ink.
- Drag anywhere to walk. Tap a rock, plant or crystal to mine it, or a creature to scan it.
- Rocks fuel your ship, plants refill your life, spiky bulbs recharge your shield, and black spires give warp shards. 40 shards build a warp cell, which jumps you to the next star.
- Name every species on a planet to pick a suit upgrade for the rest of the journey.
- Mine too greedily and the watchers come. Shoot them down for shards, or run for your ship.
- Scorched, frozen and toxic worlds wear your shield down, and stormy ones hit you with sudden storms. Your ship shelters you.
- Three journeys (4, 6 and 8 stars). Reach the core for a trophy.
- Your journey is saved as you play, and it can be added to a phone's home screen like the other Ink games.
